# TECHNICAL PROPOSAL

## 1. OVERVIEW

This proposal covers the development of a machine vision system that counts and sizes products on a conveyor belt, using cameras and an artificial intelligence model running on a server installed on site.

The work is split into stages, so that results are validated at the end of each one before the next begins.

---

## 2. SCOPE

The scope comprises:

- Installation of cameras over the conveyor.
- Recording of video from real production runs.
- Training of a detection and counting model.
- Deployment on the on-site server and commissioning.

The supply of hardware is not included in this proposal.

---

## 3. PROJECT STAGES

Estimated total duration: 5 weeks.

### Stage 1 – Survey and data collection [2 weeks]

Scope of the stage:

- Survey of the capture point and its lighting.
- Mounting and configuration of the cameras.
- Recording of real production runs.

### Stage 2 – Model training [2 weeks]

Scope of the stage:

- Assisted labelling with human review.
- Training of the detection and counting model.
- Validation against manual counts.

### Stage 3 – Commissioning [1 week]

Scope of the stage:

- Deployment of the model on the on-site server.
- Operator training and user documentation.

> Stages 2 and 3 may overlap once the first version of the model has been validated.
